SECURITY TIPS

Simple Ways to Stay Safe

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for important accounts and avoid using easily guessed information.

📲

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection to your account.

🌐

Check the Website

Before paying, check that you are using the correct website or official app.

🚨

Watch for Scams

Be cautious of messages that create urgency or ask for passwords, PINs, or OTPs.

📶

Use Trusted Networks

Avoid making sensitive payments on unknown or unsecured public networks.

🔔

Check Notifications

Pay attention to payment alerts and report anything that you do not recognize.

COMMON THREATS

Recognize Payment Scams

⚠️

Fake Payment Links

A message may contain a link pretending to be from a bank or payment service.

Tip: Don't click suspicious links.
🎣

Phishing Messages

Scammers may pretend to be trusted organizations and request sensitive information.

Tip: Verify through official channels.
💳

Fake Refunds

Someone may claim you are receiving a refund and ask for information or payment.

Tip: Check your account directly.
